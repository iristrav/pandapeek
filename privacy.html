Privacy Policy

Panda Peek: Logic Puzzle · last updated 13 November 2026

Panda Peek is made by Get Figgy, the Netherlands (“we”). We respect your privacy. Panda Peek has no accounts and we do not sell your personal data. This policy explains what happens with your data when you play.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, coins, hints, lives, bamboo, settings and language) is stored only on your own device. We do not receive it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

2. Your name in challenges

If you challenge a friend, you choose a name. This name is stored on your device and is placed in the challenge link together with the level and your time. Anyone you send the link to can see it. Choose a nickname if you prefer.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Panda Peek is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may process your IP address, device information, ad interactions and – only if you allow tracking in the iPhone pop-up – your advertising identifier (IDFA), to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the EU/UK you are asked for consent first; without consent, Google shows non-personalised ads. You can change this at any time in your iPhone settings (Privacy & Security → Tracking). More: How Google uses information from apps. Buying “No Ads” turns off interstitial ads.

4. Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases belong to your device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchases and basic device information. More: RevenueCat privacy policy.

5. Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your time for the Daily puzzle is sent to Apple's leaderboard and shown there with your Game Center nickname. This is handled by Apple: Apple privacy policy.

6. Challenge link page

Challenge links open a page hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address for security reasons: GitHub privacy statement.

7. Children

Panda Peek is suitable for all ages but is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Parents can turn off ad tracking in the iPhone settings.

8. Legal basis and your rights

We rely on your consent (advertising) and on performing our agreement with you (purchases, game features). Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to object. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, most requests are handled by deleting the app or changing your iPhone settings; for the partners above you can also contact them directly. You may file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions? Email support@getfiggyapp.com.
