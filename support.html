Support

Panda Peek: Logic Puzzle

Need help or found a bug? Email us at support@getfiggyapp.com – we usually reply within 2 working days. Please mention your iPhone model and the level number.

How do I play?

  • The numbers show how many pandas hide in each row and column.
  • Tap a square to mark it empty (✕). Double-tap to place a panda.
  • Pandas never touch each other, not even diagonally.

Lives

Every level gives you 3 bamboo sticks: a wrong panda costs one. When all 3 are gone the level fails – watch an ad for one more bamboo stick, or try again for 1 heart (❤️). You have up to 3 hearts; one grows back every hour, or watch an ad / spend 100 coins for an extra heart.

Challenges

After winning, tap “Challenge a friend” and send the link. Your friend plays exactly the same puzzle. Mistakes add 10 seconds, hints add 20 seconds. Challenges never cost lives.

My purchase is missing

Open Settings (⚙️) in the game and tap “Restore purchases”. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress; “No Ads” and the Starter Pack can always be restored.
